Multimodal Sentiment Analysis System

Major project, RSET / CUSAT, Aug 2024 to Apr 2025

A pipeline that decides how someone feels by combining what they say, how they sound and how their face looks, instead of relying on text alone.

  • Combined BERT for language, Librosa for audio features and OpenCV for facial expressions, reaching higher emotion-detection accuracy than the single-signal baselines.
  • Used cloud-based GPU acceleration to cut training and inference time, which made real-time classification across text, audio and video possible.
  • Handled preprocessing, feature engineering and model evaluation, and wrote up the findings in a full technical report.
